 
To reduce the occurrence of falsely sent fines, we need to implement validation and approval checks before sending the fines. The original process sends fines immediately after creating them without any validation, which can lead to errors and wrongful penalties. By adding a validation activity and an approval step before sending the fines, we ensure that only accurate and justifiable fines are processed.

The 'Validate Fine' activity will involve checking the fine details for correctness and compliance with predefined rules. Following successful validation, an 'Approve Fine' step will ensure managerial oversight to confirm the fine's legitimacy before it is sent.

These additions will systematically reduce the likelihood of falsely sent fines as they introduce a crucial layer of quality control to the process.
