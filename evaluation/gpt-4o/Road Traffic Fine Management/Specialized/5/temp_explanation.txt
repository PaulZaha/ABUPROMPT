 To reduce falsely sent fines, my first proposed improvement is to include a verification step before sending a fine. This step ensures the fine's legitimacy and correctness, allowing early detection and correction of any potential issues. Adding a "Verify Fine" activity as a prerequisite to the "Send Fine" activity will significantly reduce errors in sending fines. Secondly, enhancing decision-making logic can help refine the "Send For Credit Collection" step, ensuring it triggers only when fines are overdue and verified. Implementing these changes directly targets reducing falsely sent fines by improving the accuracy and verification within the decision process.
