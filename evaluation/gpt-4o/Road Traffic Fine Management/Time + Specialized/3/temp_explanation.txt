 The current process model involves multiple steps to create and send fines, insert notifications, add penalties, and manage payments and collections. To improve this process, the aim is to reduce the execution time and minimize the number of falsely sent fines. 

1. Reduce Process Execution Time:
   - Streamline the decision nodes by eliminating unnecessary exclusive choices (subprocess_2 and subprocess_3) where possible and use concurrency to speed up operations where dependencies do not strictly require sequential execution.
   - Optimize subprocess dependencies that are currently linear but can be concurrent, ensuring processes that do not depend directly on each other can be executed simultaneously.
   
2. Reduce Falsely Sent Fines:
   - Introduce an initial validation step before sending fines to catch errors early in the process. This will reduce the chance of proceeding to sending fines if there are issues detected.

The proposed improvements will ensure faster processing by leveraging concurrency and minimizing waiting times, and enhance accuracy by introducing validation early in the process to avoid the incorrect sending of fines.
