
Improvement Description:
1. **Combine related activities into subprocesses:** By creating meaningful subprocesses grouping together related activities, we streamline the execution flow. Specifically, we integrate the subprocess for inserting a fine notification and adding penalty activities together, as well as the subprocess for sending a fine and optionally executing its related subprocess.

2. **Optimize the loop execution structure:** Instead of having a self-loop structure on payment, we incorporate logic that checks payment only once and utilizes an XOR operator to handle whether the payment was successful or requires sending fines for credit collection. This reorganization should prevent unnecessarily repeating the payment step unless explicitly required by the process.

3. **Ensure payment verification before penalties:** Payment verification should happen before penalties are applied to avoid sending false fines. This requires reorganizing subprocess dependencies to check for payment completion before transitioning into steps that enforce penalties or credit collection.

These improvements contribute to achieving the improvement goals by reducing the time complexity and execution loops of the process, as well as minimizing erroneous execution that leads to false fines.
