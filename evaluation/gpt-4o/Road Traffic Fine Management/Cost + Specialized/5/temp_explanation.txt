 The given process model primarily focuses on managing fines, including their creation, notification, penalty addition, and collection. To achieve the improvement goals of reducing process execution costs and reducing falsely sent fines, the process can be improved in several ways: 

1. **Automation and Validation for Fine Creation**: Enhance the 'Create Fine' activity with automated validation checks to ensure fines are not created erroneously. This reduces the risk of sending false fines, thereby minimizing associated costs like handling disputes or corrections. Implement a validation stage before the fine is created.

2. **Streamlining Notifications and Penalties**: Combine the 'Insert Fine Notification' and 'Add Penalty' activities into a singular automated process where verification and notification are pre-validated, thus reducing manual effort and execution time costs.

3. **Optimizing Credit Collection Integration**: Postpone 'Send for Credit Collection' and link it with a final verification step ensuring that only uncontested fines proceed to this stage. This reduces the likelihood of additional administrative costs associated with collecting incorrect fines.

4. **Simplifying Payment Process**: Make the 'Payment' activity conditionally optional after the fine creation and penalty addition phases, ensuring that payments are only pursued once the correctness of the fine is guaranteed.

By implementing these improvements, we aim to minimize the likelihood of erroneously sending fines, thus reducing process execution costs from corrections, disputes, and unnecessary administrative details.
