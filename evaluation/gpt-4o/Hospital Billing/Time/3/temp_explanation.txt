 The current POWL model can be optimized to reduce process execution time by addressing unnecessary optional subprocess choices and improving concurrency wherever feasible. First, the subprocess containing the activities 'release' and 'code_ok' is executed optionally and subsequently makes 'fin' also optional due to its dependency structure. Similarly, the optionality of 'billed' may introduce delays as its execution is not mandatory. Making subprocesses that execute critical functions obligatory helps streamline the process. Additionally, enhancing concurrency by removing certain linear sequential dependencies can allow simultaneous execution of independent activities, thereby reducing total execution time. By restructuring the subprocesses to ensure necessary activities are always executed and leveraging concurrency for non-dependent activities, the overall process efficiency will improve, reducing total process execution time.
