
The process improvement focuses on streamlining decision-making and standardized care protocols to reduce execution costs and decrease wrong treatments.

1. **Streamlined Decision Paths**: Removing optional subprocesses that do not significantly impact patient treatment outcomes or improve efficiency. Optional subprocesses like `subprocess_0` containing lactic acid, are replaced with a more systematic evaluation approach that ensures quality decision-making rather than random checks.

2. **Standardized Testing and Treatment**: Merging the fragmented partial ordering in `subprocess_2`, ensuring that all critical tests (leucocytes, CRP) are run in a consistent and concurrent manner without optional pathways. Removing the convoluted `subprocess_5` architecture simplifies decisions on patient care.

3. **Concurrent Resource Allocation**: Improving the handling of resources and treatment decisions by ensuring that non-critical subprocesses optionally executed do not waste time and effort. We eliminate inefficient loops and redundant evaluation paths to direct focus on essential testing and treatments.

These improvements, along with improving process concurrency where possible, will aid in reducing the process execution costs through eliminating unnecessary testing and ensure wrong treatments are decreased by following a more rigid and structured sequence in essential part ordering.
