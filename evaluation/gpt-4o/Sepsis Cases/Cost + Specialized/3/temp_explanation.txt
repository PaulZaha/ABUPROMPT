 The current model involves a complex sequence of tests and treatments that can result in increased costs and potential wrong treatments due to the optional subprocess steps and redundant activity flows. To reduce execution cost, we should remove unnecessary or redundant options and optimize the sequence of tests and treatments. To decrease wrong treatments, we should streamline and clarify decision pathways, particularly around the triage and subsequent testing processes. This can be done by ensuring that critical tests are always performed before deciding on treatment options.
