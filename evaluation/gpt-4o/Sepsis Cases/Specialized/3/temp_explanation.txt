 In the original process model, the decision to perform certain diagnostics and treatments is modeled as optional (e.g., lactic acid test, IV antibiotics), allowing them to be skipped. This could lead to wrong treatments because crucial steps may be omitted. Additionally, critical diagnostics like leucocytes and CRP tests are concurrent, which could result in random execution order without established priorities. By making specific diagnostic activities compulsory, rather than optional, we ensure they are always considered, minimizing the chance of wrong treatments. Also, imposing a sequence upon the diagnostic steps based on medical guidelines can help with prioritization, ensuring more accurate connections between diagnosis and treatment.
