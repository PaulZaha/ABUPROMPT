 To decrease wrong treatments, we need to enhance the accuracy of the triage and ensure proper diagnosis before any treatment is provided. Key areas for improvement include enhancing initial triage and incorporating decision points or checks based on lab results before proceeding to treatment. These improvements will reduce the likelihood of errors by ensuring that all relevant information is considered before administering treatments. Specifically, we can implement checkpoints after lab results to validate the diagnosis before proceeding with any treatment and ensure that all necessary tests are performed before the final diagnosis and subsequent treatment.
