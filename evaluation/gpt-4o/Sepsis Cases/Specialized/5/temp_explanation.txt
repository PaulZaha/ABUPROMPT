 The current process model includes certain steps that can be optional, leading to variability in the treatment of sepsis patients. This optionality can contribute to wrong treatments if certain necessary steps are skipped. To decrease wrong treatments, we will eliminate optionality for critical diagnostic and treatment activities, standardize the process flow, and ensure critical diagnostics precede treatment administration. Specifically, we will make the 'Lactic Acid' measurement mandatory, reinforce the complementary use of diagnostics ('Leucocytes', 'CRP', and 'Lactic Acid'), and ensure the administration of 'IV Liquid' always precedes 'IV Antibiotics'. These changes will promote consistent diagnostic evaluations and treatment protocols, reducing the likelihood of incorrect or missed treatments.
