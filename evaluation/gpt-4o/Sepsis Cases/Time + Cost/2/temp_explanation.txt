By eliminating these redundancies, unnecessary loopbacks or assessments are reduced, hence expediting the process.

3. **Streamline Checks**: The `ER Sepsis Triage` and subsequent triages should be consolidated if information redundancies are identified, or reduced if possible to minimize time spent in assessment.
