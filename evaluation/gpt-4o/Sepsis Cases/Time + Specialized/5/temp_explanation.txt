 The existing process includes activities related to emergency registration, triage, and treatment stages involving multiple tests and treatments. To achieve the improvement goals of reducing process execution time and decreasing wrong treatments, we make the following adjustments:
1. Reorder certain activities: To enhance the efficiency of the process, we propose to execute the 'iv_liquid' and 'iv_antibiotics' jointly with 'leucocytes' and 'crp' tests. This can reduce waiting time between tests and treatment administration.
2. Mandatory Sepsis Test: The optional 'ER Sepsis Triage' step can contribute to delayed or incorrect treatment. We will redesign it as a mandatory step, ensuring all patients receive this assessment to reduce wrong treatments and improve timely diagnosis.
3. Simplify subprocess handling: By combining subprocesses into a more streamlined sequence, we can also enhance clarity with their execution reducing redundancy and potential errors.
These modifications streamline the process, reduce the likelihood of incorrect treatments, and shorten execution time.
