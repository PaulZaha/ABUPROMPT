 The current process model includes optional blood tests like lactic acid, leucocytes, and CRP, and IV treatments (liquid and antibiotics) that may be contributing unnecessary costs to the process execution. The redesign aims to streamline the process by making decisions based on triage results before proceeding with any optional tests or treatments.

To reduce costs:
1. Simplify decision points: Remove optional tests and treatments that do not have a direct impact without preceding diagnostic confirmation.
2. Reorder activities based on necessity: Ensure non-essential activities are performed only if conditions warrant them.

By making subprocess activities conditional upon explicit triage results or medical necessity rather than optional, unnecessary costs from unwarranted tests and treatments can be minimized. These changes ensure that only beneficial tests and treatments are administered, optimizing resource utilization and reducing process execution costs.
