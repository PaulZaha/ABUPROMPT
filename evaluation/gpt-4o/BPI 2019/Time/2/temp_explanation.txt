 To reduce process execution time, we will implement parallelization and remove unnecessary subprocess execution. Currently, the subprocesses for receiving order confirmation and vendor creating invoice are optional, potentially delaying the subsequent activities unnecessarily if skipped. We will ensure optional activities do not interfere with subsequent mandatory activities and attempt to run multiple activities concurrently where possible. Specifically, we will introduce concurrency for the 'Receive Order Confirmation' and 'Vendor creates invoice' activities, allowing them to execute independently and in parallel with the 'Record Goods Receipt'. This will minimize waiting time within the process flow and improve overall execution efficiency.
