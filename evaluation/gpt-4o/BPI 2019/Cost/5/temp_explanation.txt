 The current process model involves multiple exclusive choice points and optional subprocesses, which may introduce inefficiencies and increased execution costs due to potential delays and the need to handle decision-making at each xor node. To reduce process execution cost, one solution is to streamline the activities by reducing optional subprocesses and increasing concurrency where possible. We can remove or consolidate xor nodes that introduce optionality that does not significantly impact process outcomes while maintaining flexibility. Additionally, we can identify activities that can be executed concurrently to expedite the process and reduce idle times, ultimately decreasing labor costs and time to completion.
