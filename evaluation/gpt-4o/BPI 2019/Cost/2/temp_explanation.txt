 The given POWL model represents a purchase order processing workflow. The improvement goal is to reduce process execution costs. Several optimization opportunities are identified in the workflow:

1. Streamlining the Order Confirmation Process: Currently, receiving order confirmation is an optional step. Automatic confirmation can be implemented to remove this manual step, saving time and reducing labor costs.

2. Removing Optional Elements: The option to skip certain subprocesses, such as receiving order confirmation and handling invoice creation by vendors, introduces inefficiencies by allowing processes that may be necessary to be skipped, which could cause potential rework later. By mandating these steps, the workflow will become more predictable and efficient, reducing overall process costs.

3. Optimize Invoice Processing: Vendor invoice creation and the subsequent steps could be consolidated to eliminate redundant efforts. Automating parts of the invoice processing can also reduce manual work.

By implementing these improvements, we target reduced execution costs through minimizing manual interventions, removing redundancy, and ensuring efficient process flows.
