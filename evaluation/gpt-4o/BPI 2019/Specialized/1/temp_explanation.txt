 To prevent duplicate orders, I propose adding a verification step before creating a purchase order item. This step checks existing purchase orders to see if the item has already been ordered. If a duplicate is detected, the process can either bypass order creation or initiate a warning. Additionally, ensuring that the 'receive order confirmation' and 'record goods receipt' steps are clearly sequenced with the purchase order item creation helps prevent duplicates. Implementing these verification measures at the beginning of the process ensures duplication issues are avoided from the outset.
