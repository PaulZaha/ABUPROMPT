 To prevent duplicate orders, one effective approach is to enhance the order creation and confirmation phases by introducing verification mechanisms that ensure an order is not duplicated within the system. The key improvement here is to add a verification activity after the creation of purchase order items, which checks for existing, similar orders. This change ensures that before an order is officially processed, the system verifies its uniqueness. Additionally, introducing a mandatory confirmation path that is independent of optional subprocesses can further validate uniqueness before proceeding to subsequent stages.
