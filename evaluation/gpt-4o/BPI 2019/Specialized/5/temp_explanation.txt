 To prevent duplicate orders in the business process, an improvement is proposed by introducing a new activity named 'Verify Existing Orders'. This activity will precede the 'Create Purchase Order Item' action, ensuring that any new order creation is checked against existing orders. This adjustment ensures duplicate orders are identified and avoids redundant processes downstream such as order confirmation, goods receipt, and invoicing. The new process involves an exclusive choice where if a duplicate is identified at the verification stage, the 'Create Purchase Order Item' activity is skipped.
