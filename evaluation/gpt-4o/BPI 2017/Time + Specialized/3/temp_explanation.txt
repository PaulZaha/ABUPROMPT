
The original process involves multiple loops and partial orders that may cause a delay in execution time, particularly at stages like `W_Handle leads`, `W_Complete application`, and `W_Call after offers`. Additionally, the customer is kept informed only after the offer is created and sent.

To improve the process execution time:
1. **Reduce Loop Repletion for Lead Handling**: Introduce a mechanism for handling leads more efficiently to minimize unnecessary iterations.
2. **Enable Parallel Processing**: Where possible, allow concurrent execution of tasks to increase efficiency, such as processing applications alongside creating offers.
3. **Streamline Application Completion**: Ensure that once applications are completed, they are immediately processed to avoid bottlenecks.

To keep the customer informed:
1. **Introduce Communication Points**: Add activities to inform customers during critical stages, such as application submission and offer creation.

These improvements contribute towards reducing process execution time by minimizing bottlenecks and enabling concurrency, and towards keeping the customer informed by strategically placing communication activities in the process flow.
