 The original process model lacks a clear mechanism to keep customers informed throughout the process. To address this, I propose several improvements to ensure consistent communication with customers at critical points. 

1. **Introduce Notification Mechanisms**: Add activities to send notifications to customers at stages where their application status changes or when key milestones are achieved.

2. **Include Update Calls**: Integrate additional "Call after stages" activities for personalized customer engagement, particularly after critical activities such as "A_Concept" and "O_Create Offer."

3. **Enhance Optional Steps with Customer Interaction**: Ensure customers are informed even when optional paths are chosen, such as the submission or acceptance of an offer.

These changes contribute to achieving the goal of keeping the customer informed by providing immediate feedback, clarifying their application status, and maintaining engagement throughout the process.
