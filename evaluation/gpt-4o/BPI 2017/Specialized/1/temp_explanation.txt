 
To improve the process with the goal of "Keep customer informed," the focus should be on ensuring regular and timely communication with the customer throughout the process. This can be achieved by integrating communication activities at key junctures where the customer might expect updates or need to be informed about the status or any changes in the process. 

1. Add a new activity, 'Notify Customer of Application Submission,' after 'A_Submitted.' This ensures the customer is informed when their application is submitted.
2. Include a notification after completing the concept stage but before accepting the offer, 'Notify Customer of Concept Completion,' so the customer knows when the application is considered for acceptance.
3. Introduce 'Notify Customer Offer Created' after 'O_Create Offer' and 'Notify Customer Offer Sent' after 'O_Sent (mail and online).' These activities keep customers informed of offer creation and dispatch.
4. Add 'Notify Customer Application Completion' to follow 'A_Complete,' informing the customer about the process finalization.
5. Include 'Notify Customer of Cancellation' following 'A_Cancelled' to ensure customers are aware if the application cannot proceed.

These improvements will ensure that the customer is kept informed at critical points in the application process, enhancing transparency and customer satisfaction.
