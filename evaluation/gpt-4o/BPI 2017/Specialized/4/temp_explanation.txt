 To improve the process with the goal of keeping the customer informed, the key is to add communication points throughout the process. This will ensure that the customer is updated on progress at various stages. We can achieve this by including activities such as 'Notify Customer' at strategic subprocess transitions where significant progress or decisions occur. Specifically, notifications should be sent after: application submission, offer creation, offer sending, and completion or cancellation of application. This ensures the customer is informed at all critical stages.
