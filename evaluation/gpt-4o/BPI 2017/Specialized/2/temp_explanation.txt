 The existing process lacks explicit activities that keep the customer informed at various stages. To improve this, I propose adding customer notification activities after significant milestones and decisions. Specific improvements include: 1) Adding a notification activity after 'A_Submitted' to confirm the application is received. 2) Adding a notification after 'A_Concept' is generated to update the customer on the concept stage. 3) Adding a notification after offers are sent 'O_Sent (mail and online)' to inform the customer the offers are on their way. These enhancements directly contribute to the improvement goal by keeping the customer informed throughout the process.

The improvements are feasible as they do not significantly alter the complexity or execution flow, and only add simple notification tasks that can be automated.
