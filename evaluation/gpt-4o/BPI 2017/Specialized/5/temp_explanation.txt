 In the current process, customers might not be adequately informed at several critical junctures. To improve this, an explicit notification mechanism to keep the customer informed will be integrated at key points in the process. For example, after the application is submitted, when an offer is created, sent, and after processing offers. This ensures the customer is updated on progress and actions taken on their application. Furthermore, including notifications at the concept and acceptance phases will maintain transparency and improve the customer experience. These improvements are feasible considering the available resources and the process complexity.
