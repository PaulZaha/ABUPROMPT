 The original process model consists of multiple loops and optional subprocesses, which can lead to inefficiencies and increased execution costs. To reduce the process execution cost, I propose the following improvements:

1. Remove excessive self-loops: Self-loops such as in `subprocess_0`, `subprocess_1`, `subprocess_4`, and `subprocess_12` can increase operational overhead by allowing repeated execution of activities, leading to increased costs. Consolidating these loops or ensuring activities within them are performed efficiently can help reduce costs.

2. Simplify optional subprocesses: Optional subprocesses as seen in `subprocess_6` (submission handling) can incur costs from duplicated or redundant paths. It's more cost-effective to streamline submissions into a single, more direct workflow.

3. Improve concurrency: Ensuring activities that can be done concurrently are explicitly modeled in `partial_order` can minimize wait times and resource idleness, indirectly reducing execution costs.

By applying these modifications, the process will have fewer unnecessary repetitions, streamlined steps, and improved concurrency, resulting in lowered process execution costs.
