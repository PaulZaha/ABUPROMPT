Key issues found: (1) 'FIN' could occur in parallel with (and even before) verification and release, risking premature closure; (2) 'CODE OK' and 'RELEASE' were optional, enabling billing without verified codes; (3) process could be skipped after 'NEW', creating unclosed or incomplete cases. These harm external quality and create rework. Improvements made: (a) enforce 'CODE OK' as a mandatory gate before any outcome; (b) require 'RELEASE' before 'BILLED' on the billing path; (c) move 'FIN' to the end so cases are only finalized after required steps; (d) remove the raw skip-after-NEW path; provide two clean outcomes: delete, or a controlled processing path; (e) provide a fast, quality-preserving no-billing path where, once codes are verified, the case can be finalized immediately. Effects: External quality improves because billing can only occur after verified codes and release, and finalization only happens when all necessary steps are complete. Execution time is reduced by (1) short-circuiting no-billing cases directly to 'FIN' after 'CODE OK'; (2) removing ambiguity and rework caused by optional verification and premature 'FIN'; and (3) simplifying the structure to a single, gated flow that minimizes handoffs and rework loops.
