Targeted improvements to reduce time and cost: 1) Introduced an early decision gate for the sepsis pathway right after ER Triage via xor(None, sepsis_path). This makes the entire sepsis-specific work (Sepsis Triage + diagnostics + treatment) optional, preventing unnecessary execution for non-suspected cases and immediately cutting both time and cost. 2) Moved Sepsis Triage inside the optional branch so it is only performed when indicated, eliminating routine sepsis triage for all patients. 3) Parallelized diagnostics and IV fluids: after Sepsis Triage, Leucocytes, CRP, Lactate, and IV Liquid can start concurrently (no interdependencies among them). This shortens elapsed time by overlapping tasks that previously were sequenced. 4) Made each lab optional using xor(None, activity), enabling clinicians to tailor orders to the patient’s presentation and skip non-essential tests, reducing direct costs and lab waiting times. 5) Kept IV Antibiotics dependent only on IV Liquid (representing IV access), allowing faster antibiotic administration without waiting for lab completion, further reducing time-to-treatment while staying clinically safe. 6) Simplified structure and removed nested partial orders within other partial orders (the concurrency now sits inside an XOR branch), avoiding modeling complexity and ensuring all dependencies are explicit. Collectively, these changes reduce process execution time through parallelism and earlier gating, and reduce execution cost by skipping non-indicated steps.
