Key issues leading to wrong treatments in the original model: (1) The entire diagnostic/therapy bundle after ER Sepsis Triage is optional, allowing therapies without proper indication or skipping critical checks. (2) There is no explicit decision gate to separate suspected sepsis from non-sepsis patients, so antibiotics/fluids could be given unnecessarily. (3) Safety checks are missing: no allergy/renal-dose verification before antibiotics, no culture-before-antibiotics requirement, and no contraindication check before fluids. (4) A rigid sequence IV Liquid -> IV Antibiotics could encourage fluids even when contraindicated and is not required for correctness. Targeted improvements and how they reduce wrong treatments: - Add an explicit XOR after ER Sepsis Triage to separate the Sepsis path from a Non-sepsis observation path. This prevents antibiotics/fluids when sepsis is not suspected, directly reducing inappropriate treatments. - Introduce Sepsis Protocol Activation as the first step in the sepsis branch to standardize care via an order set and anchor all subsequent actions to a clear indication. - Make core diagnostics (Leucocytes, CRP, LacticAcid) mandatory within the sepsis path (but concurrent, so they do not delay therapy). This reduces misclassification-driven treatment errors without harming timeliness. - Require Blood Cultures to precede IV Antibiotics. This avoids wrong/untargeted therapy and preserves diagnostic accuracy while maintaining speed (cultures can be drawn in parallel with other actions). - Insert Antibiotic Safety & Selection before IV Antibiotics. This consolidates allergy check, local guideline selection, and renal/weight-based dosing to prevent allergy-related errors and wrong antibiotic choice/dose. - Add an optional Pharmacist Verification before antibiotics via xor(None, pharmacist). This introduces a second safety barrier when available without delaying care (optional). - Add Contraindication Check for IV Fluids before IV Liquid. This reduces wrong fluid administration (e.g., CHF, ESRD patients). - Remove the rigid sequence IV Liquid -> IV Antibiotics. After the required checks, fluids and antibiotics can proceed independently, ensuring antibiotics are not delayed by fluids and fluids are not given when contraindicated. Feasibility: These changes reflect standard sepsis protocols and ED practices. Diagnostics and safety checks are concurrent and do not block urgent therapy. Pharmacist verification is optional to fit staffing realities. The resulting model keeps throughput high while creating multiple safety gates to prevent wrong treatments.
