Targeted changes to reduce wrong treatments: 1) Introduced a gating decision after ER Sepsis Triage via an exclusive choice. If sepsis is suspected, the standardized sepsis bundle is executed; if not, the patient proceeds to a general physician assessment without sepsis-specific treatments. This prevents inappropriate antibiotics/fluids in non-sepsis cases. 2) Standardized care through a Sepsis Order Set to reduce variation and ensure correct evidence-based steps. 3) Mandatory safety checks before antibiotics: Allergy Check and Renal Function Check must precede Antibiotic Selection and Dose. This reduces wrong-drug and wrong-dose events, especially in renal impairment and allergies. 4) Pharmacist Verification and Barcode Medication Administration Check are enforced prior to IV Antibiotics. This creates independent professional verification and a bedside electronic check to prevent wrong medication, wrong patient, or wrong dose administration. 5) Blood Cultures must precede IV Antibiotics, preserving diagnostic accuracy and enabling targeted therapy, reducing mismatched treatments. 6) Fluid Contraindication Check must precede IV Liquid, reducing harm from inappropriate fluid boluses (e.g., in heart failure). 7) Lactic acid is now mandatory within the sepsis path, aligning with sepsis severity assessment and avoiding under/over-treatment due to incomplete data. The updated POWL enforces these controls via partial-order dependencies, ensuring antibiotics cannot be administered unless all prerequisite checks and cultures are completed, and that fluids are only given after contraindication screening. The XOR gating ensures the sepsis bundle is not triggered for patients without confirmed suspicion, directly decreasing wrong treatments.
