Key issues in the original model causing long execution time and false fines: (1) Credit collection could be triggered even if the fine was never sent; (2) Notification and penalty were optional and loosely sequenced; (3) Payment was forced to occur at least once via loop(payment, None); (4) No upfront validation of fine data, recipient identity/address, or duplicate/prior payment checks.

Targeted improvements and how they meet the goals:
- Front-loaded automated validation, executed in parallel: Validate Fine Data, Verify Recipient & Address, and Check Duplicate / Prior Payment all start right after Create Fine and run concurrently. This shortens elapsed time before decision while dramatically reducing erroneous fines.
- Risk-based manual review as an optional step: Only flagged cases go to Manual Review; others skip it. This reduces overall cycle time while preventing false sends for suspicious cases.
- Explicit decision gate to either Cancel Fine or proceed with sending: If validations or review fail, the process terminates via Cancel Fine. This directly reduces falsely sent fines.
- Enforce correct ordering: All downstream activities (notification, reminders, penalty, eligibility check, and credit collection) are reachable only if the fine was actually sent, eliminating the prior possibility of sending to credit collection without a sent fine.
- Immediate, consistent record-keeping: Insert Fine Notification always occurs after sending, improving traceability and reducing downstream rework.
- Parallel follow-up: Payment attempts and reminders are modeled as skippable self-loops (0..n) running in parallel. This reduces waiting and speeds up resolution for fast payments while avoiding unnecessary steps for non-payments.
- Penalty applied only after the reminder/payment loops conclude: This reduces false penalties by ensuring sufficient follow-up occurred before penalty application.
- Formal eligibility check before credit collection: Check Credit Collection Eligibility gates the optional Send for Credit Collection, preventing wrongful escalations (e.g., already paid, disputed, or invalid fines).

Net effect:
- Reduced execution time through parallel validations and parallel reminders/payments, optional review only when needed, and removal of unnecessary paths.
- Reduced falsely sent fines through robust pre-send validation, the explicit cancel path, and eligibility gating before credit collection.
