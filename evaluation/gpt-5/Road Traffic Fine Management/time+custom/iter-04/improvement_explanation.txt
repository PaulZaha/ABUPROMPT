Targeted improvements to reduce execution time and reduce falsely sent fines:
- Add a pre-send verification gate: Introduced 'Verify Fine (auto + manual for flagged)' to run automated rules (duplicate detection, evidence completeness, plate-to-vehicle registry match, address/contact validation) and route only flagged cases to manual review. This cuts false positives before they reach 'Send Fine'. A dedicated 'Reject Fine' path terminates invalid cases early, preventing any downstream work and eliminating falsely sent fines.
- Parallelize pre-send preparation: Moved 'Insert Fine Notification' before 'Send Fine' and executed it concurrently with a new 'Prepare Digital Payment Link'. 'Send Fine' now depends on both being ready. This reduces wait time and ensures citizens receive a fine with a working payment link, accelerating payment and shortening overall cycle time.
- Replace the unbounded payment loop: Removed the self-loop on 'Payment' to avoid indefinite rework. Post-send, the process now branches either to 'Payment' or (if unpaid) to 'Check Due Date & Payment Status'. This simplifies flow and reduces throughput time.
- Guard penalty and collections with a status check: Added 'Check Due Date & Payment Status' ahead of penalty and escalation. Penalty application is modeled as an optional step ('xor(None, Add penalty)') that occurs only when due-date has passed and payment is not recorded. This reduces wrongly applied penalties and prevents premature collections.
- Eliminate optional sending: In the previous model 'Send Fine' was optional. It is now performed only in the validated path, ensuring that downstream steps (payment/collections) are never triggered for fines that were not properly sent.
Together, these changes combine strong upfront validation, smart parallelism, and cleaner post-send decisioning to lower false fines and shorten the end-to-end processing time.
