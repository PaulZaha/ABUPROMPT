Cost reduction is achieved by: (1) removing the unbounded self-loop on Payment (which incurs repeated transaction fees and handling) and replacing it with a capped number of payment attempts (1 or 2) via an XOR; (2) deferring and consolidating the pre-emptive penalty flow (Insert Fine Notification -> Add penalty) so it only occurs if we escalate to collections, and merging those two tasks into a single step (Notify and Apply Penalty) to cut handoffs and processing costs; (3) gating the expensive Send for Credit Collection so it is only reachable after the lower-cost self-service payment attempts, avoiding early, costly external collection fees; and (4) standardizing the send step to a low-cost digital channel and keeping it optional so we avoid postage/print when cases are cancelled early. These changes are feasible as a policy/configuration update: cap payment retries, default to digital communications, and execute penalty/notification only when escalating. The new control flow preserves essential outcomes while eliminating wasteful repetitions and unnecessary manual steps, thereby lowering average execution cost per case.
