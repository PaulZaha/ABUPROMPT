Key cost-reduction changes applied to the process: 
- Removed the manual 'Insert Fine Notification' step and integrated it as an automated system update triggered by existing steps. This eliminates labor time and duplicated handling without impacting compliance.
- Made 'Send Fine' mandatory and placed a single decision directly after it: either the fine is paid or the case proceeds to penalty and then credit collection. This removes ambiguous skip paths that caused exceptions and rework.
- Replaced the payment self-loop with a single payment path. Multiple manual payment attempts create repeated processing and transaction costs; by limiting to one, we reduce back-office and channel costs. Late or partial payments are handled via the credit collection process.
- Consolidated the non-payment route into a minimal sequence ('Add penalty' -> 'Send for Credit Collection'), executed only when payment does not occur. This ensures penalty and collection activities are not performed unnecessarily, reducing average execution cost per case.
Overall, the streamlined structure reduces the number of activities executed per case, removes redundant manual steps, and eliminates rework-heavy branches, directly lowering staff time, system usage, and third-party handling costs.
