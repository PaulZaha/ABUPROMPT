Targeted improvements and why they help: 1) Add automated gate to prevent false fines: Introduced two low-cost automated checks (Validate Fine (rules+dedup) and Verify Identity & Address) plus an optional Manual Review if Flagged. If any check fails, the flow goes to Cancel Fine. This directly reduces falsely sent fines (wrong party, duplicate, data errors) and prevents downstream costs (printing, postage, handling disputes, collections rework). 2) Eliminate illogical paths and rework: In the original model, credit collection could occur without reliably sending the fine. The redesigned model enforces Send Fine before any reminder/penalty/collection, preventing wrongful collections and associated fees. 3) Order reminders before penalties: Insert Fine Notification now precedes Add penalty, giving a chance to settle before costlier penalty and collection steps, reducing disputes and processing costs. 4) Make payment optional and flexible: Switched to a skippable self-loop for Payment (0..n). This removes the prior implicit requirement to have at least one payment event, which could drive unnecessary processing/handling costs in non-payment cases. 5) Gate credit collection with a final automated check: Added Pre-collection Payment/Dispute Check before optionally sending for Credit Collection. This avoids sending paid or actively disputed cases to collection, reducing chargebacks, vendor fees, and reputational risk. 6) Keep cost efficiency: Manual Review is optional (only if flagged), while all other added checks are automated and inexpensive relative to the avoided downstream costs. Payment can occur in parallel with reminder/penalty sequencing to reduce cycle time without adding manual effort. Net effect: fewer false fines, fewer wrongful collections, and lower execution cost from avoided rework, postage, and vendor fees, with minimal added automation cost.
