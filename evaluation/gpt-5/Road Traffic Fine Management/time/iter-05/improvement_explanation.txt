Targeted redesign decisions to reduce overall execution time:
- Enable early payment: Previously, payment (and credit collection) had to wait until after the optional "Send Fine" and notification/penalty branch. Now, payment starts immediately after "Create Fine" and runs in parallel with sending the fine and inserting the notification. This shortens the critical path for on-time payers.
- Remove unbounded retries: Replaced the self-loop on "Payment" (1..n attempts) with a single payment attempt. This eliminates long-running cases due to repeated retries and forces a quick resolution decision, reducing cycle time.
- Immediate resolution path: After the payment attempt, we immediately decide to either end (paid) or escalate. The escalation branch bundles "Add penalty" followed by "Send for Credit Collection" so the case moves to collection without unnecessary waiting.
- Parallelize non-critical comms: "Send Fine" and "Insert Fine Notification" are made optional and concurrent with the payment attempt. This reflects digital channels (where explicit sending may be skipped) and avoids blocking the main flow, cutting lead time.
- Flatten sequencing: The top-level is a single partial order with explicit dependencies to avoid hidden sequential constraints and enable maximum safe concurrency.
These changes are feasible in practice: payment can be initiated instantly via online channels, communications can be handled asynchronously, and penalties are applied only if escalation is needed, minimizing work on cases that pay promptly while accelerating non-payment resolution.
