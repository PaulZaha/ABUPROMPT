Key changes focus on eliminating rework, decoupling follow-ups from finalization, and enabling safe concurrency, which together reduce both execution time and cost. 1) Removed the self-loop on W_Complete application; executing it once avoids costly rework cycles. 2) Made W_Handle leads skippable and repeatable from 0..n via a skippable self-loop, triggered after A_Submitted; this implements risk-based triage (skip handling for low-risk cases) and limits mandatory touches, cutting cost and lead time. 3) Decoupled follow-up calls from completion/cancellation by modeling follow-ups as a skippable self-loop (0..n) that occurs after the offer is sent, and finalization happens once after calls; this prevents unnecessary post-finalization calls, shortening end-to-end time and reducing wasted effort. 4) Kept A_Concept optional, but ensured it precedes offer creation when present; this reduces unnecessary concept work while preserving necessary dependency. 5) Flattened nested partial orders into a single final partial order to avoid hidden synchronization overhead and ensure clean, correct dependencies. Overall, the redesigned control flow removes unnecessary loops, reduces mandatory work, and allows parallel/non-blocking progress where feasible, which decreases cycle time and operational cost.
