Key issues found: (1) three unbounded self-loops (W_Handle leads, W_Complete application, W_Call after offers) cause rework and waiting, inflating time and labor cost; (2) A_Submitted was optional, which triggers incomplete data early and more rework; (3) excessive sequentialization (no parallelism) lengthens cycle time; (4) the loop around follow-ups and terminal outcomes could lead to repeated work even after completion/cancellation.

Improvements implemented to reduce execution time and cost:
- Cap rework instead of unbounded loops: replaced all three self-loops with bounded alternatives using xor plus copy to allow at most one extra pass. Concretely: W_Complete application and W_Handle leads are now 1 or 2 passes (never unlimited). Follow-up calls after offer are limited to 0, 1, or 2 attempts. This directly cuts labor time and effort spent on overprocessing and repeated touchpoints.
- Parallelize early work: after A_Submitted, W_Handle leads (optional, max 2 attempts) can run in parallel with W_Complete application (1–2 passes). This shortens end-to-end lead time without adding resources.
- Make A_Submitted mandatory: removed the optional submission branch to ensure basic data quality upfront and avoid unnecessary loops/hand-offs later. This reduces rework and clarifies flow.
- Gate follow-ups behind Offer Sent: calls are only part of the offer path and flow after O_Sent (mail and online). If there’s no offer, no calls are made, eliminating waste.
- Clean terminalization: replaced the loop that entangled follow-ups with terminal outcomes by a clean xor between A_Complete and A_Cancelled -> O_Cancelled. This prevents additional iterations after a terminal event.
- Structural simplification: avoided partial order nested inside another partial order at the top level; partial orders are used only within xors where needed, reducing modeling ambiguity and execution overhead.

Operational rationale: Capping rework is a standard policy change (e.g., max one rework pass, max two follow-up attempts). Parallelizing lead handling and application completion removes idle time. Mandatory submission ensures that downstream tasks start with sufficient information, reducing clarifications. Overall, these changes reduce hands-on time, cycle time, and failure demand, while preserving optional business paths (concept/offer) and required controls.
