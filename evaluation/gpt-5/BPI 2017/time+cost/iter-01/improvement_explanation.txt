Key improvements to reduce execution time and cost while keeping the flow feasible and controlled:
- Remove unbounded rework loops: The original model allowed unlimited repetitions of W_Handle leads, W_Complete application, and W_Call after offers. These were replaced by a capped rework pattern (one mandatory pass plus at most one optional rework via xor and copy), which sharply reduces repeated manual effort and rework-induced waiting times.
- Introduce straight-through processing (STP) for lead handling: W_Handle leads is now optional and executed only when triage rules require it. Low-risk or auto-qualified cases bypass manual handling, cutting average processing time and labor cost.
- Eliminate the nested call/completion loop: Replaced with a simple optional call after sending the offer. This avoids repeated call cycles and prevents additional iterations after completion, directly lowering effort and cycle time.
- Execute acceptance/offer activities only when completing the case: The acceptance and offer chain is moved inside the completion branch of the final xor. This prevents preparing offers for cases that will be canceled, avoiding unnecessary work and cost.
- Keep A_Concept optional and upstream: When needed, it precedes acceptance, but it does not force extra steps if the case will be canceled.
Operational impact: These changes reduce the number of manual touches, remove rework churn, and simplify the path to completion. They also preserve controls (e.g., if a follow-up call is performed, it must occur after the offer is sent and before completion) while enabling a faster no-call completion route when customer confirmation is available.
