- Removed unbounded loops that created rework and long tails in cycle time: replaced all self-loops (W_Handle leads, W_Complete application, W_Call after offers) with bounded choices (1–2 times for leads and completion; 0–2 for follow-up calls). This cuts excessive rework, lowers labor cost, and reduces variance in case durations.
- Parallelized pre-offer work: after A_Submitted, W_Handle leads and W_Complete application can proceed concurrently. This directly shortens the critical path, reducing end-to-end processing time without adding resources.
- Made A_Submitted mandatory (removed optionality). This prevents skipping a pivotal milestone, reducing exception handling and rework downstream.
- Gated follow-up calls to only occur when an offer was actually sent and limited to at most two attempts; also allowed zero calls if digital delivery suffices (e.g., customer opened the offer). This removes wasteful contact attempts and reduces cost while preserving responsiveness where needed.
- Simplified and linearized the middle section by removing the nested do/redo loops that forced returning to W_Complete application after the offer stage. The flow is now: complete pre-offer work (in parallel) -> optional concept -> optional offers branch -> single final decision (complete or cancel). This avoids cycling and shortens throughput time.
- Preserved all original activities and core business semantics (at least one execution of leads handling and application completion; cancellation still triggers O_Cancelled). Calls only happen if O_Sent (mail and online) occurred, eliminating non-value-adding steps.
Overall impact: fewer rework cycles, less manual effort (bounded retries), and overlapping tasks produce a shorter critical path and lower labor cost per case, while keeping the process feasible and compliant.
