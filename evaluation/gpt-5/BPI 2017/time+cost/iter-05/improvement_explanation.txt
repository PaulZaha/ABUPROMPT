Key improvements to reduce execution time and cost: 1) Removed unbounded loops on lead handling, application completion, and post-offer calls. Unbounded loops create rework, waiting, and repeated manual effort, inflating cycle time and cost. Replaced them with bounded, optional steps (at most one follow-up on leads, at most one rework on completion, and at most one post-offer call). 2) Consolidated offer creation, creation confirmation, and sending into a single automated activity (O_Generate and Send Offer). This eliminates handoffs and queueing between multiple offer-related steps, reducing latency and effort. 3) Ensured downstream processing only occurs for submitted applications by gating the entire processing chain behind an XOR that skips the chain when the application is not submitted. This prevents cost-incurring work on non-submitted applications. 4) Kept concept optional but enforced order: concept precedes acceptance, acceptance precedes offer generation and sending. This maintains feasibility while avoiding unnecessary steps when concept is not needed. 5) Structured cancellation as a simple, sequential subchain only executed in the final XOR, removing ambiguous looping behavior after completion/cancellation. Together, these changes simplify the flow, eliminate rework cycles, reduce human touches, and prevent work on non-viable cases, leading to faster throughput and lower operational cost.
