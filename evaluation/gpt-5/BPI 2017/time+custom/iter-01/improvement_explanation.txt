Key issues identified in the current model: (1) A_Submitted is optional, which is unrealistic and creates ambiguity; (2) the loop structure around post-offer calls and completion is inverted, causing unnecessary rework and potential infinite cycling; (3) lead handling and application completion are serialized, elongating throughput time; (4) customer communication is sparse and not aligned to key milestones. Targeted improvements to reduce execution time and keep the customer informed: 
- Make submission explicit and confirm receipt: A_Submitted is now mandatory and immediately followed by O_Confirm Submission. This reduces inbound inquiries and improves transparency without adding delay.
- Parallelize early work to shorten cycle time: W_Handle leads and W_Complete application now run in parallel after submission confirmation. This removes unnecessary waiting and shortens the critical path.
- Keep customer informed during processing: Introduced an optional, non-blocking O_Status Update loop (0..n) that can be used when needed without gating downstream tasks.
- Keep concept optional but non-blocking: A_Concept remains optional and can proceed in parallel with early work. Acceptance depends on the chosen concept path being resolved but does not block other parallel work.
- Correct loop misuse and remove rework after completion: Replaced the previous loop that forced re-execution after completion/cancellation. Post-offer calls W_Call after offers are now an optional 0..n loop after O_Sent (mail and online), so calls no longer add mandatory delay.
- Start notifying while producing the offer: Upon A_Accepted, O_Notify Acceptance runs in parallel to O_Create Offer (followed by O_Created and O_Sent), keeping the customer informed without delaying offer preparation.
- Clear and informed outcomes: The end of the process is modeled as an XOR between A_Complete and a cancellation path that informs the customer (A_Cancelled -> O_Cancelled -> O_Inform Cancellation).
Impact on goals: 
- Reduce process execution time: Parallelization of lead handling and application completion, elimination of mandatory post-offer calls, and removal of mis-modeled loops that re-trigger after completion reduce overall cycle time. Optional steps are preserved but no longer gate progress unnecessarily.
- Keep customer informed: Added O_Confirm Submission, O_Notify Acceptance, O_Inform Cancellation, and an optional O_Status Update loop. Notifications are aligned to key milestones and designed to run concurrently to avoid delays.
