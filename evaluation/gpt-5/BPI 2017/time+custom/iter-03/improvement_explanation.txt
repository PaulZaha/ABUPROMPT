Key changes aimed at reducing execution time and keeping the customer informed:
- Make submission mandatory and acknowledge it immediately: Removed the optional submission path and added an immediate auto-confirmation activity after A_Submitted. This reduces rework and starts all subsequent work and communication without delay.
- Parallelize early work: Start W_Handle leads and W_Complete application concurrently right after submission. This reduces overall lead time by overlapping internal workstreams.
- Skippable iterations to avoid unnecessary work: Converted W_Handle leads and W_Call after offers to skippable self-loops (0..n) so they can be omitted when not required, cutting time in straightforward cases.
- Proactive status updates: Added O_Progress Update as a skippable self-loop that can run in parallel while the application is being worked on, keeping customers informed during processing without gating the flow.
- Clarify and streamline the offer and decision stages: Kept A_Concept optional, followed by A_Accepted, then O_Create Offer -> O_Created -> O_Sent (mail and online). Replaced the previous do/redo loop structure with a simple sequence: send offer, optionally perform follow-up calls, and then decide (A_Complete or cancellation). This prevents unnecessary cycling and speeds up closure.
- Final customer notification: Added O_Final Decision Notification after either completion or cancellation to ensure customers are informed of the final outcome.
These improvements are feasible with standard CRM/workflow automation (auto-acknowledgements, periodic updates) and by enabling teams to work in parallel where dependencies allow. They reduce waiting times, remove mandatory rework loops, and add timely customer communications at key milestones.
