Key improvements and how they meet the goals: 1) Reduce execution time: a) Eliminate unbounded rework loops. The original model allowed indefinite repetitions for W_Handle leads, W_Complete application, and W_Call after offers via self-loops, which can balloon cycle time. We replaced each with an optional single rework attempt using xor(None, task.copy()). This caps rework to at most one additional pass, removing uncontrolled loops while still allowing pragmatic corrections. b) Parallelize post-submission work. After A_Submitted, we run W_Handle leads, W_Complete application, and automated confirmation concurrently. This minimizes idle time and accelerates the transition to the acceptance/offer stage. c) Introduce an early cancellation gate. After post-submission work, we added a decision xor to either proceed or cancel early (A_Cancelled -> O_Cancelled). Early termination prevents unnecessary downstream steps (concept, acceptance, offer creation/sending, call follow-ups), significantly reducing overall cycle time for non-viable cases. d) Streamline post-offer follow-up. We replaced the unbounded loop of calls with a single optional follow-up call, enabling quicker progression to final outcome (complete or cancel). 2) Keep customer informed: a) Automated touchpoints are added at key milestones: C_Submission Confirmation after submission; C_Acceptance Notification after acceptance; C_Offer Sent Notification after offer dispatch; and final outcome notifications (C_Completion Notification or C_Cancellation Notification). These activities run in parallel where possible and immediately after their triggering events, ensuring timely, proactive updates to the customer throughout the process. 3) Structural clarifications: a) Submission is enforced (removed the optional submission path) to avoid downstream inconsistency and rework. b) Concept remains optional and correctly precedes acceptance; acceptance triggers both the offer pipeline and customer notification concurrently. c) The model uses partial orders to express concurrency and essential sequencing without nesting partial orders unnecessarily, while xor captures optional/rework behaviors and decision gates. Overall, the redesign reduces waiting and unnecessary repeats, introduces early exits, and embeds timely customer communications, achieving faster throughput and better customer awareness.
