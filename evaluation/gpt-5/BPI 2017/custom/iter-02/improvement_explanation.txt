To keep the customer informed end-to-end without disrupting the core routing, I added explicit customer communication activities (prefixed with 'C_') at all key milestones and embedded periodic updates into iterative parts of the process. These are feasible as automated notifications triggered by existing system states/tasks.

Key changes and how they help:
- After application creation and submission, send immediate confirmations so customers know their request was received and is being worked on.
- During iterative lead handling and application completion, send status updates each iteration so customers see progress and are not left in the dark; optionally request additional information when needed.
- After concept (pre-decision), notify the customer that the application is under review.
- On acceptance, before offer creation, notify acceptance; after offer creation and send, notify the customer that the offer is created and sent (including online availability).
- After each follow-up call attempt, notify the call outcome (e.g., reached/voicemail) to maintain transparency.
- On final outcomes (completion or cancellation), send definitive notifications.

Design considerations:
- Notifications are added as sequential steps after the relevant activities or inside loop bodies, ensuring they occur reliably without altering the original decision logic.
- Optional steps (like requesting additional info) are modeled with xor(None, ...), keeping them conditional.
- The existing loops and choices remain intact, with notifications complementing them to improve customer communication.

Overall, these changes provide proactive, consistent touchpoints throughout the process, directly supporting the goal of keeping the customer informed.
