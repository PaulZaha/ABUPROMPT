To keep the customer informed throughout the process, I introduced targeted communication and portal-update activities at key milestones and during waiting/rework loops. Specifically: 1) Immediate acknowledgments and status updates: after application creation and submission, the customer receives confirmations and the portal is updated. 2) Periodic status updates during waiting phases: while leads are handled and while the application is being completed, we now send repeatable status updates and reminders modeled via loop redo parts, ensuring proactive communication without altering core work steps. 3) Offer-related notifications: when an offer is created and before/after it is sent, the customer is notified and the portal is updated, increasing transparency. 4) Follow-up and finalization: calls after offers now generate follow-up emails; finalization paths (complete or cancel) trigger appropriate customer notices and portal updates. 5) Structural correction for calls/finalization: the previous loop forced additional calls after finalization; I replaced it with an optional call loop followed by the finalization choice, so communications stop appropriately once the case is complete or canceled. These changes are feasible (they introduce only messaging/status activities and minor restructuring of existing loops) and directly improve customer awareness at all stages without disrupting the operational logic.
