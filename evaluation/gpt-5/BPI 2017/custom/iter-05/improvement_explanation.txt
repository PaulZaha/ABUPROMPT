To keep the customer informed throughout the process, I introduced explicit customer communication activities at key status changes and embedded them within the existing loops so that updates are consistently sent after each iteration of internal work. Concretely: (1) After A_Submitted, the customer receives an immediate confirmation (C_Notify Submission Received). (2) Each cycle of lead handling (W_Handle leads) is followed by a status update (C_Update Lead Handling Status), by restructuring that loop to include the update. (3) Each iteration of application completion work (W_Complete application) is followed by a customer update (C_Update Application Progress). (4) If a concept is produced (A_Concept), the customer is notified (C_Notify Concept Shared). (5) After acceptance (A_Accepted), the customer is notified (C_Notify Acceptance) before offer creation proceeds. (6) Each call after offers is followed by a written summary (C_Send Call Summary). (7) On completion (A_Complete) and cancellation (A_Cancelled/O_Cancelled), the customer is informed (C_Notify Completion, C_Notify Cancellation). These changes maintain the original control-flow (optional branches and loops) while ensuring timely, consistent communication at every critical step, thereby achieving the goal of keeping the customer informed without disrupting feasibility.
