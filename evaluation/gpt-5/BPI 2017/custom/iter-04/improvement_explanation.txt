To keep the customer informed throughout the process, I introduced explicit customer communication activities and positioned them at key milestones and within iterative work steps so updates happen consistently and without adding unnecessary overhead. Specifically: 1) After A_Submitted, the process now sends a confirmation (C_Confirm Submission) before lead handling begins. 2) The iterative lead handling (W_Handle leads) is wrapped so each iteration is followed by a customer status update (C_Status Update (Leads)). 3) The iterative application processing (W_Complete application) is similarly wrapped so each iteration triggers a status update (C_Status Update (Processing)). 4) After offers are sent, the follow-up calls loop (W_Call after offers) is augmented with a customer-facing status update (C_Post-Offer Update) after each call. 5) After the final decision (either A_Complete or cancellation via A_Cancelled -> O_Cancelled), the customer receives an outcome notification (C_Notify Outcome). I also simplified the control flow by making post-offer calls precede the final decision rather than looping back after completion/cancellation, ensuring communications occur at the right time. These adjustments preserve the original optionality and looping behavior but embed communication steps that provide clear, timely information to the customer, directly addressing the goal of keeping the customer informed.
