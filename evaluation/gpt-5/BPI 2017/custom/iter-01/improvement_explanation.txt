To keep customers informed throughout the process, I introduced dedicated customer communication activities at key milestones and embedded status updates directly into iterative work loops. Concretely: - After A_Submitted, the customer receives an immediate acknowledgement. - Each iteration of W_Handle leads and W_Complete application now ends with an automated status update to the customer, ensuring they are regularly informed during processing. - If a concept is selected (A_Concept), the customer is informed. - When an offer is created and sent, the customer is notified that the offer is ready and then receives a send confirmation. - Each post-offer call iteration concludes with a customer-friendly summary of the call outcome. - Final outcomes include explicit customer notifications: completion confirmation after A_Complete, and cancellation notice after O_Cancelled. I also corrected the post-offer loop structure to avoid the prior modeling where completion/cancellation were embedded as loop redo steps (which could lead to repeated calls after finalization). Calls after offers are now modeled as a self-loop that can repeat 1..n times and are made optional as a whole segment, followed by a clear exclusive choice between completion or cancellation, each with corresponding customer communication. This preserves feasibility, keeps the original intent (optional concept and offers), and ensures timely, consistent customer updates without disrupting the core operational flow.
