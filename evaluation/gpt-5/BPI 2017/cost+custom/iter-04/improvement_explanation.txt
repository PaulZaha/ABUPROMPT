Key improvements to reduce cost and keep customers informed: 1) Replace repeated manual follow-ups with automation: We removed the costly self-loop of W_Call after offers and introduced an automated O_Send Reminder loop (0..n) after the offer is sent. We also limit the manual W_Call after offers to at most once via an optional XOR. This significantly reduces labor while maintaining engagement. 2) Limit expensive lead handling: We changed W_Handle leads from a mandatory 1..n loop to an optional one-time step (XOR), cutting repeated manual touches. 3) Automate customer communications at critical touchpoints: Added O_Acknowledge Submission right after A_Submitted to confirm receipt and O_Completion Confirmation after A_Complete to close the loop with the customer. O_Cancelled remains in place for clear cancellation notice. 4) Streamline control flow and remove problematic nested loops: We flattened the model into a single partial order with clear, necessary sequences and well-scoped optional/looped components. W_Complete application is now 0..n, reflecting that further completion work may or may not be needed, avoiding forced effort. 5) Preserve essential business logic while enabling concurrency: After A_Submitted, acknowledgement, optional lead handling, and completion work can proceed concurrently, reducing wait and rework time. Offer creation remains a clean sequence after (optional) concept and acceptance. Together these changes remove redundant work, reduce manual outreach costs, and add automated, timely customer notifications for improved transparency.
