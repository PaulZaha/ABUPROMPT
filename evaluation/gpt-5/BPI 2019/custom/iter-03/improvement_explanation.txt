To prevent duplicate orders, I introduced a mandatory pre-creation gate and a controlled decision point. The new activity 'Check for Duplicate Order' is executed before any order creation. Based on this check, an XOR directs the process either to a 'Link to Existing Order' branch (when a duplicate is detected) or to a 'Create' branch. In the duplicate branch, the process ends after linking to the existing order, ensuring that no new order is created or processed further in this case. In the create branch, I added 'Approve Order Creation' as a mandatory step before 'Create Purchase Order Item' to enforce a second check or supervisory confirmation that no duplicate exists. The rest of the flow (order confirmation, goods receipt, and invoice processing) remains as in the original model to preserve operational behavior. This design makes it impossible to execute 'Create Purchase Order Item' when a duplicate is found and adds an approval control to reduce human error, both of which directly prevent duplicate orders.
