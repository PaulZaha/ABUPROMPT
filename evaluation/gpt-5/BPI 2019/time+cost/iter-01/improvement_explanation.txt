Key improvements and how they reduce time and cost: 1) Removed the unnecessary gating of downstream work by Receive Order Confirmation. Previously, Record Goods Receipt and invoice-related steps were blocked by the optional order confirmation. Now, these can progress as soon as the PO item is created, shortening lead times. 2) Enabled earlier recording of invoice receipt by making it depend only on the vendor creating the invoice (not on goods receipt). This allows AP to capture invoices as soon as they arrive and process them in parallel with warehouse activities, cutting waiting time. 3) Strengthened payment controls to avoid costly rework: Clear Invoice now requires both Record Invoice Receipt and Record Goods Receipt. This enforces a 3-way match condition (PO/GR/invoice) before payment, reducing exception handling and manual interventions. 4) Kept Remove Payment Block optional and explicitly placed it after invoice receipt. This ensures the manual block removal happens only when needed (mismatch cases), reducing effort and cycle time. 5) Flattened the model to a single partial order (no nested partial orders), which makes dependencies explicit and avoids missed constraints, improving robustness. Operationally, to handle orders that historically lacked an invoice (e.g., free-of-charge items), standardize by auto-recording a zero-value invoice in Record Invoice Receipt. This keeps the flow consistent without adding manual work. Overall, these changes increase concurrency, eliminate unnecessary waits, and reduce manual exceptions—directly improving execution time and cost.
