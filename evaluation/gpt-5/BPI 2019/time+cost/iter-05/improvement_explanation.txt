Key improvements to reduce time and cost: 1) Enable early and parallel processing: Record Invoice Receipt now runs in parallel with Record Goods Receipt immediately after PO creation. This removes the prior gating that delayed invoice processing until the goods receipt/vendor step completed, reducing idle time and shortening the critical path. 2) Make Receive Order Confirmation non-blocking: It remains optional but is executed concurrently after PO creation, avoiding unnecessary waits while still capturing confirmations when available. 3) Introduce Automated 3-Way Match: After both the goods receipt and invoice receipt are available, an automated match is performed. If it passes, payment can proceed immediately; if not, the process falls back to manual Remove Payment Block. This minimizes manual work and exception handling, lowering processing cost and time-to-payment. 4) Retire non-value-adding internal dependency on Vendor creates invoice: This external action is no longer modeled as a blocking/internal step; we focus on the controllable internal flow to avoid artificial sequencing and wait states. 5) Flatten sequencing with a single partial order: The model avoids nested partial orders and encodes only necessary dependencies, preventing unintended constraints. Expected impact: shorter end-to-end cycle time due to concurrency and reduced manual work through touchless invoice verification, with feasibility ensured by standard e-invoicing and automated 3-way match capabilities; manual removal remains as a safe fallback.
