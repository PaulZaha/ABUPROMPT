Key changes to reduce execution time and cost: 1) Remove unnecessary gating: Previously, invoice processing (record invoice receipt, remove payment block, clear invoice) was forced to wait until the concurrent block of goods receipt and vendor invoice creation completed. We rewired the dependency so the invoice processing can start as soon as the vendor creates an invoice. This reduces waiting time and shortens the critical path. 2) Increase parallelism: After PO item creation, Receive Order Confirmation (optional), Goods Receipt, and Vendor Invoice Creation (optional) can progress independently. This eliminates idle time caused by sequential handoffs and allows different teams/systems to work in parallel, reducing overall cycle time. 3) Preserve optional steps to avoid unnecessary work: Receive Order Confirmation, Vendor Invoice Creation, and Remove Payment Block remain optional. This ensures that in cases where confirmations or payment block removals are not needed, the process skips them, lowering execution cost. 4) Simplify structure (flatten nested partial orders): We removed the nested partial order for concurrency and expressed dependencies directly at the top level. This avoids coordination overhead and reduces the risk of implicit waits, helping time and cost. Together, these changes enable earlier invoice capture, faster downstream processing, and avoid performing non-value-add activities when not needed.
